Privacy — beta

2026-09-14

This notice describes the beta. Legal bases, provider retention periods and international transfer safeguards remain to be finalized before public release.

Who develops Arka?

Arka is a beta habit journal developed by Théo Faucon. For questions about your data, contact theofaucon06@gmail.com. This notice describes the features in the September 14, 2026 beta.

Your journal on this device

Your local first name, habits, start dates, completions, bookmarks and selected photos are stored on your device. Photos are chosen through the system picker; Arka does not browse your entire photo library. When you are signed in with Apple, your progress photos are also copied to Arka’s private storage (Supabase, European Union), readable only by your account, and restored after a reinstall or on another device. Without an account they stay on the device only, so uninstalling the app may cause their loss.

Optional Sign in with Apple

Signing in is optional. Apple supplies a sign-in identifier and may provide account information according to your choices. Arka requests your name; the authentication service may also receive an email associated with the Apple token. Supabase synchronizes your profile, bio, chapters, habits and progress so you can retrieve your journal. Do not enter medical details or other sensitive information in the beta’s free-text fields.

Usage measurement

Amplitude receives usage events including setup steps, chapter creation, opening offers, progress milestones and video generation. Its SDK uses a device identifier and technical information and may infer approximate location from the connection. Events may be associated with your account identifier when signed in. The current configuration uses Amplitude’s United States region. These events help identify problems and improve the app; they are not used to display advertising.

Subscriptions and testing

Apple and RevenueCat manage offers, receipts and subscription entitlements. Arka does not receive your payment card details. This version uses a RevenueCat identifier associated with the installation. Purchases from a TestFlight build use Apple’s test environment; Apple displays the applicable details.

Providers and hosting

Providers are Apple for authentication, TestFlight and StoreKit; Supabase for authentication and synchronized data in a European project region; RevenueCat for subscriptions; and Amplitude for analytics in its United States region. These services may process connection and technical data. This version has no advertising, squads or social feed.

Retention and deletion

Local data stays on the device until deleted or the app is uninstalled, subject to system backups. An account can be deleted through Gallery → Account → Delete my account. This removes account data managed by Arka and does not cancel an Apple subscription. For provider-held data such as analytics events or receipts, contact theofaucon06@gmail.com. Detailed retention periods and safeguards for international transfers must still be formalized before public distribution.

Your rights and choices

You can use the journal without an account. Contact theofaucon06@gmail.com to request access, correction, deletion or portability, or to exercise an applicable right to object or restrict processing. You may also contact the CNIL at www.cnil.fr. This beta makes no automated decisions that produce legal effects about you.

Scope of this beta notice

This notice will be updated if features or data processing change. The app owner must finalize the legal basis for each processing activity, provider retention periods and international transfer mechanisms before public distribution. The notice describes the beta’s current behavior for testers; it is not a certification of complete legal compliance.
