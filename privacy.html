Privacy

2026-10-12

Who is responsible for your data?

Arka is a habit and wellness journal developed by Théo Faucon, the data controller. Contact: theofaucon06@gmail.com. This notice applies to Arka 1.0 starting with build 9. Earlier beta versions used automatic usage measurement; build 9 turns it off by default.

Your journal on your iPhone

Your local first name, habits, dates, checkmarks, favorites, preferences and photos are stored on your device. You can use the journal without an account. Photos are selected through the system picker; Arka does not browse your entire photo library. Memory videos are created on-device and shared only when you request it. Local data remains until you delete it or uninstall the app, subject to system backups.

Optional sign-in and synchronization

If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple provides an identifier and the account details you authorize, including your name and potentially an email or relay address. Supabase synchronizes your profile, bio, challenges, habits, progress and journey photos. The primary project is hosted in the EU West region, Ireland. Photos are kept in private storage accessible by the signed-in account. Synchronization provides the service you request and relies on contract performance (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Some habits and images may reveal information about your wellbeing. Arka does not request medical records, diagnoses or access to Health / HealthKit. Avoid entering medical or other sensitive information in free-text fields.

Usage measurement with your permission

Usage measurement is optional and off by default. In Gallery → Account, “Help improve Arka” lets you give or withdraw consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). Refusal does not limit any feature. If you agree, Amplitude receives technical events such as viewing an offer, starting a challenge, onboarding steps, milestones and creating a video. It uses a random measurement identifier, without your account identifier, name, photos or journal content. Automatic events and Amplitude geolocation are disabled. A network connection technically exposes an IP address to the server, but the SDK requests that it not be stored as an event property or used for geolocation. There is no session recording, advertising or cross-app advertising tracking.

Withdrawing consent and deleting analytics

Turn off the setting to stop future events and clear the queue stored on this device. A request already in flight may finish. Withdrawal does not automatically erase events already received by Amplitude and does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing. To request earlier erasure, copy the measurement ID shown in Account before turning the setting off, then contact us. Events have a three-month retention period from ingestion in Amplitude, enforced through provider deletion. The initial purge may take up to thirty days; genuinely anonymous aggregates may be retained longer.

Purchases and Arka Pro

Apple processes payment; Arka does not receive your bank card details. RevenueCat receives an installation identifier, purchased products, receipts, dates, statuses and technical data needed to verify, restore and manage Pro access. Processing relies on subscription performance (Article 6(1)(b)), applicable legal obligations and legitimate interests in fraud prevention and handling claims. Data is retained while managing entitlements and restoration requests; you can request deletion of the RevenueCat profile, except data strictly needed for a legal obligation or dispute. Those exceptions depend on the relevant obligation or limitation period. Apple retains its own records under its rules. Deleting the app or Arka account does not cancel an Apple subscription. RevenueCat also provides aggregate purchase statistics to manage the service and check offers, based on the legitimate interest in operating the service, without targeted advertising.

Account and support retention

Your synchronized profile, journal and photos remain while you use the account or wish to keep the journal, until deletion. Account deletion removes Arka-managed account data and associated private files. The current daily database backup window is seven days (Supabase Pro). Backups expire under that window and are isolated from normal use; they are not used to reactivate deleted accounts. Deletions must be reapplied after any technical restoration. Deleted photo objects are not restored by a database backup. Support messages are kept until resolution and then for up to twelve months, except legal obligations or disputes. Privacy requests and completion evidence may be kept for three years with restricted access to demonstrate compliance. Provider security logs follow their respective technical and legal retention rules and are not reused for advertising.

Recipients and international transfers

Recipients are Théo Faucon and necessary providers: Apple for sign-in and payment, Supabase for accounts, data and photos, RevenueCat for purchases and, only with consent, Amplitude for usage measurement. Primary EU hosting does not exclude support access or technical processing outside the EU. Amplitude uses its United States region, and RevenueCat may process data in the United States. Provider processing agreements specify applicable transfer safeguards, including European Commission standard contractual clauses and, where its conditions apply, an adequacy mechanism. Contact us for further information or a copy of safeguards. Provider documents: supabase.com/legal/dpa; revenuecat.com/dpa; amplitude.com/dpa. Arka does not sell your journal or photos to data brokers or use them to train AI models.

Security

Service connections use HTTPS. Account data and photos are subject to access rules restricted to their owner. Administration access is limited to operational and support needs. No system guarantees absolute security. Protect your iPhone and Apple account and contact us about any unusual access.

Your rights and account deletion

You can request access, correction, erasure, portability and, depending on the legal basis, restriction or objection. You may withdraw optional measurement consent at any time. In the app: Gallery → Account → Delete my account. For purchase or measurement data, email theofaucon06@gmail.com, including the identifier shown in Account if available so we can locate the relevant profile. Never send passwords or bank documents. Proportionate identity verification may be required. We respond within one month, subject to a lawful extension for a complex request. You may complain to the CNIL at www.cnil.fr or your local supervisory authority.

Audience and automated decisions

Arka is intended for people aged 16 and over. Wellness content is not a diagnosis or medical treatment. Arka does not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you. If we learn that a child has provided data outside applicable conditions, we handle deletion requests with their legal representative.

Notice updates

This notice is dated 12 October 2026. Purpose changes or new consent requirements will be presented before they apply where a new choice is needed.
